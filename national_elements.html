National flag

The National Flag is a Horizontal tricolour flag of India saffron (kesaria) at the top,white in the middle and India green at the bottom in equal proportion

National Anthem

The song Jana-gana-mana, composed originally in Bangla by Rabindranath Tagore

National song

The song Vande Mataram , cpomposed in Sanskit by Bankimchandra Chatterji,was a source of insoiration to the people in their stuggle for freedom. It has an equal status with Jana-gana-mana

National bird

The India peacock,pavo cristatus, the national bird of India, is a colourful,swan-sized bird, with a fan-shaed crest of feathers, a white patch under the eye and a long, slender neck
